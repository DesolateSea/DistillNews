India sub-junior men beat Pakistan to win Youth Hockey5s Asian Championship

Source: PTI   -  Edited By: Laxmi Negi
Updated: July 26, 2026 13:20 IST • 2 Minutes Read
x

India's sub-junior men's hockey team displayed exceptional skills to defeat arch-rivals Pakistan 3-1 and win the gold medal at the FIH Youth Hockey5s Asian Championship and qualify for the World Cup.

IMAGE: India's triumphant sub-junior men's hockey team. Photograph: Hockey India/X

Key Points

  • India's sub-junior men's team won gold at FIH Youth Hockey5s Asian Championship.
  • India defeated arch-rivals Pakistan 3-1 in a thrilling final.
  • Goals by Romit Pal, Ketan Kushwaha, and Shahrukh Ali secured India's victory.
  • Both India and Pakistan qualified for the inaugural FIH Youth Hockey5s World Cup.
  • India's sub-junior women's team won a silver medal and Hockey India announced cash rewards for both the teams.

The Indian sub-junior men's team produced a clinical performance to defeat arch-rivals Pakistan 3-1 in the gold medal match of the FIH Youth Hockey5s Asian Championship in Muscat on Saturday.

Goals from Romit Pal (5'), captain Ketan Kushwaha (12'), and Shahrukh Ali (13') crowned India as the Asian Champions. With this victory, the Indian men's team also finished at the top of the Elite Pool table.

India's Dominant Performance And World Cup Qualification

The summit clash saw India take early control with sharp passing and disciplined structure.

Romit Pal (5') broke the deadlock in the first half, striking past the Pakistan goalkeeper to give India a 1-0 lead at half-time. India extended their dominance early in the second half as captain Ketan Kushwaha (12') found the back of the net, followed immediately by a strike from Shahrukh Ali (13') to make it 3-0.

 

Pakistan pulled a goal back through Muhammad Usman (16'), but the Indian defense stood firm in the closing minutes to secure a 3-1 victory and lift the trophy. By virtue of reaching the final, both India and Pakistan qualified for the inaugural FIH Youth Hockey5s World Cup.

In the women's competition, the Indian sub-junior women's team bagged the silver medal after a hard-fought contest in their final against China, ensuring both Indian contingents return home with medals.

Celebrating the stellar performances of both squads, Hockey India announced cash rewards for the teams. Each player of the gold medal-winning Indian men's team will be rewarded with Rs 2 lakh, while each member of the support staff will receive Rs 1 lakh.

Additionally, each player of the silver medal-winning Indian women's team will receive Rs 1 lakh, with Rs 50,000 awarded to each member of their support staff.
