Good afternoon!

It’s track and field day at the Commonwealth Games. 

Athletics is arguably the grandest event on the Glasgow roster where a couple of Indian stars, including former World and Olympic champion Neeraj Chopra, will be expected to come home with medals.

Before we get there, here’s a small introduction. My name is Santadeep Dey, and I will be taking you through the live action. Our correspondent, K. Keerthivasan, is at the venue to provide us with timely updates.
