Global pricing for the Redmi Note 17 series leaks

Vlad, 27 July 2026

The Redmi Note 17 and Redmi Note 17 Pro were launched in China earlier this month, and we recently heard the former would land in India on August 6. But of course the range is also headed to other markets, including Europe. Pricing in euros for the devices has now been leaked.

The Redmi Note 17 4G will allegedly cost €250 with 4GB of RAM and 128GB of storage, while the 6GB/256GB version will set you back €300. The Redmi Note 17 5G with 6GB of RAM and 256GB of storage will go for €400.

Chinese Redmi Note 17

The Redmi Note 17 Pro 5G with 6GB of RAM and 256GB of storage will be priced at €500, while the Redmi Note 17 Pro Max 5G with 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage will be yours for a whopping €600, apparently.

The source of this leak says the global Redmi Note 17 will have a 108MP main camera and will be offered in black, blue, and purple, but doesn't explicitly state if this is the 4G model or the 5G one. We assume the latter, but we'll see. It's also unclear what other differences there will be between the Chinese models in the range (as there's just two of them) and the much expanded global lineup.

But if the global launch is taking place sooner rather than later, then we expect to be able to fill in all these blanks in a few weeks at most. Stick around and we'll keep you posted.
