London Spirit get on board with thrilling win over Trent Rockets

by Cricbuzz Staff •  
Chasing a target of 143, Marie Kelly top-scored with an unbeaten 58 off 33 © Getty
Chasing a target of 143, Marie Kelly top-scored with an unbeaten 58 off 33 ©Getty

Trent Rockets vs London Spirit

London Spirit secured a thrilling five-wicket win after Nadine de Klerk smashed Ashleigh Gardner for a six off a full toss with 11 needed off the final seven balls. Chasing 143, Grace Harris (37) and Amy Jones (21) gave Spirit a flying start with a 55-run opening stand before both departed in quick succession. Their dismissals triggered a collapse as Spirit slipped from 55/0 to 67/4 after also losing Marizanne Kapp and Sterre Kalis. Marie Kelly and de Klerk then combined for a decisive 63-run partnership to revive the chase, before de Klerk sealed the win in style with an unbeaten 41.

Opting to bowl, Kapp gave the visitors an early breakthrough by dismissing Beth Mooney for a golden duck. Sophia Dunkley and Sophie Luff steadied Trent Rockets with a brisk 33-run stand, but Kapp returned to remove both batters and finished with figures of 3-12. Captain Gardner then provided the impetus through the middle with a 22-ball 34, which included five fours, before Bess Heath added late runs with an unbeaten 31 off 19. The Rockets were 95/4 when Gardner fell on the 63rd ball and failed to find the finish they were after, with Georgia Elwiss (13 off 16) even being retired out in a bid to raise the scoring rate.

Brief Scores: Trent Rockets 142/7 in 100 balls (Ashleigh Gardner 34, Bess Heath 31*; Marizanne Kapp 3-12) lost to London Spirit 146/5 in 97 balls (Nadine de Klerk 41*, Marie Kelly 39; Ashleigh Gardner 2-24) by five wickets.
