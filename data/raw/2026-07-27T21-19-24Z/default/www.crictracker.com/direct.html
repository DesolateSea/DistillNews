The Hundred Men's 2026: Match 9, SOB vs MIL Match Prediction – Who will win today’s Hundred match between Southern Brave vs MI London?

Both teams are coming off losses in their recent matches.

By CricTracker Staff

Published - 27 Jul 2026, 18:00 IST

6 Min Read

Southern Brave (SOB) takes on MI London (MIL) in Match No. 9 of the Hundred Men's 2026 at The Rose Bowl in Southampton on Monday, July 27. Both teams are coming off losses in their recent matches.

The Brave have started their campaign with two losses and suffered a nervy 5-run defeat to the Sunrisers Leeds at Headingley. They conceded a total of 187 for 5 in their 100 balls after bowling first, and missed out on the total despite runs from Jamie Smith, Ben McKinney and Tristan Stubbs.

MI London started their campaign off with a victory against the Sunrisers, but were beaten by the Welsh Fire in Cardiff, losing by 60 runs. They conceded a total of 167 for 5 courtesy of a stellar 98 from Rachin Ravindra. They were unable to chase the total despite James Vince's stellar 66 at the top, losing by 15 runs.

Spinners are expected to play a significant role on this surface, being helped by grip and turn.
