The Xiaomi 18 Pro might not be a China exclusive after all, new listing reveals: Reports

By Mudassir Kamran
Modified Jul 26, 2026 18:55 IST

The Xiaomi 18 Pro could finally break away from Xiaomi's long-standing strategy of keeping its Pro flagship exclusive to China. A newly spotted global database listing suggests the handset is being prepared for multiple international markets, hinting at a wider release than expected.

While Xiaomi has yet to confirm anything officially, the certification has sparked fresh speculation that the upcoming flagship may soon reach users outside China.

Xiaomi's decision to keep its Pro flagship models exclusive to China may finally be changing. Fresh evidence suggests the upcoming Xiaomi 18 Pro is heading to global markets, giving international buyers access to a flagship that was previously unavailable outside China.

The latest certification and database listings have strengthened speculation that Xiaomi is preparing a much wider rollout for its next premium smartphone.

According to XimiTime, the 18 Pro has appeared with the global model number 26122PN61G, while the Chinese version carries the model number 26122PN61C.

ADVERTISEMENT

The publication also claims the device, codenamed VENICE, is expected to launch in several regions, including Turkey, Europe, Russia, Taiwan, Japan, and other global markets. However, the report adds that India may not be part of the initial rollout.

While Xiaomi has not officially confirmed the Xiaomi 18 Pro's global debut, multiple recent leaks point in the same direction. It looks like Xiaomi is planning a broader international release for the Xiaomi 18 lineup than in previous years.

If the reports prove accurate, the 18 Pro could become one of the company's first Pro-branded flagships to receive a large-scale global launch, giving users outside China another premium option in Xiaomi's flagship portfolio.
