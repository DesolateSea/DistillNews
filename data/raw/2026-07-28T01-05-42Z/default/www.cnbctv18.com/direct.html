Vivo S2 India launch tipped for August 6; design, specs and sale date revealed

Vivo S2 may launch in India on August 6, with sales starting August 11. It could feature a Dimensity 7360 chip, 1.5K AMOLED 120Hz display, and 50MP OIS camera.

By CNBCTV18.com  July 27, 2026, 4:39:35 PM IST (Published)
2 Min Read
The Vivo S2 could make its India debut on August 6, according to recent leaks, signalling the return of the company's S-series smartphones after nearly six years. Ahead of the expected launch, multiple reports have revealed the handset's likely sale date, colour options, design and several hardware specifications.



According to tipster Abhishek Yadav, the smartphone is expected to go on sale from August 11.

EXCLUSIVE ✴️

Launch: August 6
First Sale: August 11 pic.twitter.com/IiLr2TuoAp

It is tipped to arrive in 8GB + 128GB and 8GB + 256GB storage variants, according to a report in GSMArena.com. It may be offered in Regal Bronze, Sapphire Blue and Silk White colour options.

Separately, dummy units shared by tipster PassionateGeekz offer a closer look at the phone's design. The images suggest the Vivo S2 could feature a curved display and a centrally positioned hole-punch selfie camera. The handset is also expected to have a polycarbonate frame and rear panel.

Vivo S2 5G dummy Units arrived!!

- 50MP OIS + 8MP UW
- 1.5K AMOLED 120hz#Vivo #VivoS2 #VivoS2series pic.twitter.com/pF3U2bqscO

In terms of specifications, the latest leak points to a 1.5K AMOLED display with a 120Hz refresh rate, powered by a MediaTek Dimensity 7360 chipset. The rear camera setup is tipped to include a 50-megapixel primary sensor with optical image stabilisation (OIS) and an 8-megapixel ultra-wide camera.

ALSO READ |Why affordable smartphones are struggling in India while premium devices hold up better

Earlier reports painted a different picture, claiming the device would feature a Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 processor and a triple rear camera system comprising a 50-megapixel main sensor, a 50-megapixel periscope telephoto camera, and an 8-megapixel ultra-wide lens. Reports also suggested the handset could pack a 7,000mAh battery.

According to tipster Abhishek Yadav, demo units of the smartphone could be priced at around ₹38,000 and ₹42,000, while the 8GB + 128GB variant has been listed at ₹43,000. "Internal retail listings show demo units priced at ₹38,000 and ₹42,000. The 8GB+128GB retail listing is currently showing ₹43,000. These are internal listing prices, not the final launch prices," he shared on X earlier.

However, Vivo has not officially confirmed the launch date, specifications or pricing of the rumoured handset.
