Rs 75 dividend per share: Tata Group company's HIGHEST-EVER reward announcement in Q4 results 2025

Rs 75 dividend per share: The Q4 quarterly results announcement season has started with companies reporting their earnings for the January-March 2025 period. After TCS, Tata Elxsi has become the second Tata Group company to announce its financial results for the period under review.
Photo : ET Now Digital

Rs 75 dividend per share: Tata Group company's HIGHEST-EVER reward announcement in Q4 results 2025

With this, the BSE 200-listed company has also announced its highest-ever dividend of 750 per cent. Tata Elxsi has also shared an update on the payment of the cash reward.

Rs 75 Dividend Per Share

While declaring its earnings for the last quarter of the financial year 2025, the Tata Group company has announced a 750 per cent dividend payout, which translates to Rs 75 per share at a face value of Rs 10. “We are pleased to inform you that the Directors have recommended a dividend of Rs 75 per equity share of Rs 10 each, for the financial year 2024-25.”

Tata Elxsi Dividend 2025

The Tata Group company further noted that the Rs 75 cash reward shall be paid on or after the seventh day from the conclusion of the 36th Annual General Meeting, subject to the approval of the shareholders of the Company. This is the highest amount every announced by Tata Elxsi.
For FY24, the Tata company had declared a dividend of Rs 70 per share, up from Rs 60.60 per share declared for the financial year 2023. In FY22, Tata Elxsi board had recommended a final dividend of Rs 42.50 per share.

Tata Elxsi Q4 Results 2025

For the fourth quarter of financial year 2024-25, the Tata company reported operating revenue of Rs 908.3 crore while the full year revenue was Rs 3,729 crore. Tata Elxsi’s revenues from operations came in at Rs 908.3 crore, while EBITDA at Rs 207.7 crore and EBITDA margin at 22.9 per cent.
Tata Elxsi’s profit after tax for the January-March 2025 period came in at Rs 172.4 crore while PAT margin at 18.1 per cent.
