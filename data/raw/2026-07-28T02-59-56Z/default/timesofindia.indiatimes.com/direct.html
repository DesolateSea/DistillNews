'Good Bad Ugly', directed by Adhik Ravichandran and starring Ajith Kumar and Trisha Krishnan, continues its stellar performance at the global box office even after eight days of its release. The action-packed entertainer, which opened to largely positive reviews, has managed to sustain strong collections. As per the latest update from Sacnilk.com, the film has now minted a whopping Rs 194 crore worldwide, adding another Rs 5 crore on its eighth day.
Becomes highest-grossing Tamil film of the year
In India, 'Good Bad Ugly' has earned Rs 113.85 crore net and Rs 134 crore gross in the first seven days. Star-studded cast and gripping storyline draw audiences
The film features a powerhouse ensemble cast including Ajith Kumar, Trisha Krishnan, Arjun Das, Sunil, Karthikeya Dev, Priya Prakash Varrier, Prabhu, Prasanna, Tinnu Anand, and Raghu Ram. In the film Ajith as AK is also known as "The Red Dragon," a feared Mumbai gangster who surrenders to the police in 2007 to leave his violent past behind and live peacefully with his wife Ramya and son Vihaan. After spending 18 years in prison, AK is released with the hope of reuniting with his family, who now live in Spain. However, his plans are disrupted when Vihaan is wrongfully arrested on drug charges linked to a mysterious shooting incident.
