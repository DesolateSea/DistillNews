Frenkie de Jong & Marc-Andre Stegen included in Barcelona squad for pre-season trip to England

A few surprises in the travelling party.

by Gill Clark
Jul 27, 2026, 9:58 AM UTC
AFP via Getty Images

Barcelona have named a 30-man travelling squad for their pre-season training camp in England that will feature friendlies against Birmingham City and Preston.

The players are due to fly out shortly and will head to England’s training base at St George’s Park which will be their home for the next week.

Marc-Andre ter Stegen is on the list, despite being in talks to leave on loan for Ajax, while Frenkie de Jong is also in the squad but won’t play due to a knee injury.

Here’s a look at the full squad list.

Goalkeepers: Marc-Andre ter Stegen, Aron Yaakobishvili, Wojciech Szczęsny, Iker Rodríguez.

Defenders: Hector Fort, Alejandro Balde, Ronald Araujo, Alvaro Cortés, Xavi Espart, Andreas Christensen, Gerard Martin, Jofre Torrents, Jordi Pesquer.

Midfielders: Guille Fernández, Brian Fariñas, Tommy Marques, Fermín Lopez, Marc Casadó, Marc Bernal, Ibrahim Diarra, Frenkie de Jong, Orian Goren.

Forwards: Hamza Abdelkarim, Toni Fernández, Alex González, Ebrima Tunkara, Karim Adeyemi, Roony Bardghji, Shane Kluivert, Òscar Gistau.

The camp will see Hansi Flick get the chance to continue to check on a raft of impressive young stars as well as new signing Karim Adeyemi.

Fermin Lopez and Ronald Araujo are also part of the squad as they work their way back from injuries.
