2026 F1 Hungarian Grand Prix - Budapest results

Check out the full results from the Hungarian Grand Prix – held at the Hungaroring

Nick Golding
26 July 4:43PM
0 comments 0
Add us as preferred source
Article
To news overview © 2026 Getty Images

Lando Norris converted pole position into victory in the Hungarian Grand Prix, despite losing the lead early on to Oscar Piastri. 

Piastri led the opening stages before losing the lead mid-race, only to then retire from the race after a gearbox problem. 

Max Verstappen and championship leader Kimi Antonelli completed the podium, while Lewis Hamilton crossed the line in fourth but was demoted to fifth. 

A five-second time penalty for speeding in the pit lane promoted his teammate Charles Leclerc to fourth.
