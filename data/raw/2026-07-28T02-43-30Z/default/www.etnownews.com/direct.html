    ICICI Bank Q4 results, dividend 2025 announcement date and time, expectations, preview, estimates

    ICICI Bank Q4 Results, Dividend 2025 Announcement: ICICI Bank Q4 result 2025 date is April 19. ICICI Bank dividend announcement is also scheduled to take place in the Q4 results. ICICI Bank dividend 2025 record date may be announced along with among in quarterly results.

    Written by: Abhinav Ranjan

    Updated Apr 19, 2025 06:53 IST

    ICICI Bank Q4 results, dividend 2025 announcement date and time, expectations, preview, estimates (Pic: ET NOW)

    ICICI Bank Q4 Results, Dividend 2025 Announcement: ICICI Bank, the country's second largest private bank, will announce its quarterly results (Q4 FY2025) today (April 19). ICICI Bank could also announce a final dividend for shareholders in the Q4 results.
    According to an exchange by ICICI Bank, a board meeting is scheduled to take place on April 19 (Saturday) to consider and approve the quarterly results or Q4 results. ICICI Bank Q4 results could be announced in afternoon.

    ICICI Bank Dividend 2025 Announcement

    The board of ICICI Bank will also take up a proposal for the declaration of dividend. If dividend is announced by ICICI Bank, then this will be final dividend by the private bank for FY2025.
    "Meeting of the Board of Directors of ICICI Bank Limited is scheduled to be held on Saturday, April 19, 2025, to, inter alia, consider and approve the audited financial results (standalone and consolidated) for the quarter and year ending March 31, 2025 and recommendation of dividend, if any, for the financial year ending March 31, 2025," ICICI Bank said in an exchange filing.
    ICICI Bank dividend record date was not announced in the latest exchange filing. ICICI dividend record date may be announced along with amount in the Q4 results FY2025.
    ICICI Bank has a dividend yield of 0.71 per cent on each stock. ICICI Bank has paid dividend once every year since 2021. In 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024, ICICI Bank rewarded its investors by paying then a dividend of Rs 2, Rs 5, Rs 8 and Rs 10, respectively, on each stock.

    ICICI Bank Earnings Call Time

    ICICI Bank has said that it will hold an earnings call on April 19 evening. ICICI Bank earnings call will commence at 5 PM.
    "The Bank will host an earnings call with analysts and investors at 5:00 p.m. (IST) on April 19, 2025 to discuss the financial results of the Bank for the quarter and financial year ended March 31, 2025," ICICI Bank said in a separate filing.

    ICICI Bank Q4 Results Expectations, Preview

    According to Sharekhan, banks are expected to deliver yet another muted quarter with modest earnings growth in Q4FY25.
    On ICICI Bank, Sharekhan said that advances are likely to grow by 15% YoY, aided by broad based growth in all segments. The NIMs, which is used to measure a bank's profitability by analysing interest income vs expenses, is expected to lower marginally on QoQ basis.
    Sharekhan said that it expects ICICI Bank to report a standalone PAT of Rs 12,032 crore, up 12.4 per cent YoY and 2 per cent QQ. The bank's NIIs, difference between interest income and interest expense, is seen increased b7 8.5 per cent YoY and 1.7 per cent QoQ to Rs 20.176 crore in the quarter under preview.
    The PPoP (pre-provision operating profit) is pegged at Rs 25,995 crore in the fourth quarter, down 11.2 per cent YoY and up 4 per cent QoQ. ICICI Bank, Sharekhan said, is relatively better positioned as asset quality would remain healthy.
    Key monitorables in ICICI Bank Q4 results would be deposit growth and margins outlook.
