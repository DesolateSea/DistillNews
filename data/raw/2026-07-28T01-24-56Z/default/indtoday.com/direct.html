    Hyderabad, July 25: The gold rates today in Hyderabad rise on 27 July, 2026. Going by the rates today, the price of 10 grams of 22-carat gold is Rs. 1,33,700 with a rise of Rs. 850 while the rate of 10 grams of 24-carat gold is Rs. 1,44,920 with a fall of Rs. 10.

    While the Silver rate is at Rs. 2,40,000 per kilogram.

    The gold rates in the international market have been fluctuating. Over the past few weeks gold rates have experienced a fall during the wedding season, fluctuating around Rs. 1,40,000 for 10 grams of 24-carat gold and approximately Rs. 1,50,000 for 10 grams of 22-carat gold.

    The gold prices mentioned here are due at 8am, the prices could alter at every moment and hence the gold buyers need to track the live prices at a given time.
