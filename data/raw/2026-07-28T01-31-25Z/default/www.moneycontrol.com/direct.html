      This smallcap tea stock nearly hit 20% upper circuit on latest govt notification: Check details

      The rally followed a communication from the Ministry of Heavy Industries urging all central ministries and departments to promote the use of Yule Tea.
      Paras Bisht
      July 27, 2026 / 13:38 IST
      Andrew Yule shares rise in trade.
      • Andrew Yule & Co. shares rose nearly 20%.
      • Centre mandated 'Yule Tea' for official use.
      • Directive aims to support the state-run company.

      Shares of state-run Andrew Yule & Company rose nearly 20 percent during intra-day trade on Monday after the Centre asked all ministries and departments to serve 'Yule Tea' at official meetings, conferences, canteens and government functions.

      The stock climbed 19.65 percent to an intra-day high of Rs 30.50 per share on the BSE. It later pared some gains and was trading at Rs 28.11, up 10.28 percent, at around 1 pm, as investors booked profits at higher levels.

      The stock has been gaining over the last two trading sessions.

      The rally followed a communication from the Ministry of Heavy Industries urging all central ministries and departments to promote the use of Yule Tea, the tea brand owned by Andrew Yule & Company Ltd (AYCL), at official meetings, conferences, canteens and government functions.

      According to the ministry, AYCL, a Central Public Sector Enterprise under its administrative control, owns and operates tea estates in Assam and West Bengal and is among the country's oldest tea producers.

      The company manufactures Darjeeling, Dooars and Assam teas in orthodox, CTC, green tea and flavoured variants under the 'Yule Tea' brand. It also supplies tea to Parliament through the Tea Board, while its products are available online.

      The ministry said the company's products have been well received by customers. However, it added that AYCL is facing financial stress due to various reasons.

      It also said the Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee on Industry had recommended coordinating with ministries and departments for wider promotion and adoption of Yule Tea.

      The ministry said greater institutional support and wider use of the company's products would provide significant encouragement to its ongoing efforts towards operational and financial stability.
