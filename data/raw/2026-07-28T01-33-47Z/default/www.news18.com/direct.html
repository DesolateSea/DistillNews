Do you need expensive kitchen gadgets to cook healthy? These affordable essentials under Rs 1,000 can help

Curated By :
  • Mayank Jha
  • ,CNN-News18
Last Updated:July 27, 2026, 19:39 IST

Healthy cooking doesn't need expensive appliances. These affordable kitchen gadgets can help with portion control, steaming and low-oil meal preparation at home.

1/7
Small gadgets can make healthier cooking easier
Preparing healthier food does not necessarily require an air fryer, food processor or another expensive appliance. Boiling, poaching, steaming and measuring portions are simple practices that can support balanced home-cooked meals.
Here are some affordable kitchen gadgets available on Amazon that can simplify these everyday tasks. Prices and availability may vary depending on the seller and delivery location.
Advertisement
2/7
AGARO Grand Egg Boiler and Poacher
The Agaro egg poacher and boiler is a two-in-one appliance that can boil up to eight eggs or poach up to four eggs at a time.
It offers three boiling settings and can also steam suitable vegetables. The 500W appliance has a stainless-steel body and a transparent lid for monitoring the food while it cooks.
3/7
Nutripro Multipurpose Cook Kettle
The Nutripro Multipurpose Cook Kettle is a 600W compact appliance supplied with an egg tray and stainless-steel steaming rack. It can boil eggs, steam vegetables, heat water and prepare simple soups or other suitable one-pot meals.
Its steaming and boiling functions provide alternatives to oil-heavy cooking, while the compact design may suit small kitchens and hostel rooms. Buyers should check the listed capacity, warranty and cleaning instructions before ordering.
Advertisement
4/7
Atom Aliston Digital Kitchen Scale
It helps measures ingredients in one-gram increments and supports up to 10kg.
The scale can help users measure grains, vegetables, meat, snacks and cooking ingredients instead of estimating quantities by sight.
5/7
Prepare eggs without frying them
Egg boilers make it easier to prepare boiled or poached eggs without using oil. Their automatic operation also reduces the need to monitor a saucepan continuously.
Users can select their preferred level of doneness, although results may vary according to the number, size and starting temperature of the eggs.
Advertisement
6/7
Measure portions more consistently
A digital kitchen scale can support portion control by providing a more consistent measurement than cups, spoons or visual estimates.
It can be useful when following a meal plan, tracking ingredients or preparing measured servings. However, individual dietary requirements should be determined with guidance from a qualified health professional.
7/7
What to check before buying
Buyers should check the capacity, power rating, safety features, cleaning requirements, warranty and return policy before ordering an electrical kitchen appliance.
For a kitchen scale, check its measurement accuracy, maximum supported weight and battery requirements. Amazon prices, sellers and product availability can change without notice.
