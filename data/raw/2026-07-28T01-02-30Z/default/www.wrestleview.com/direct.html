Bayley wrestles at WWE house show on Saturday

July 26, 2026

A posted footage of Bayley wrestling at WWE’s Saturday house show at the Adventist Health Arena in Stockton, California.

Bayley competed in a triple threat match against Sol Ruca and Lyra Valyria in a triple threat match.

This comes after it was reported on Saturday from Dave Meltzer at The Wrestling Observer, there was talk backstage following last weekend’s Saturday Night’s Main Event, the match Bayley had against Valkyria may have been Bayley’s last time wrestling at Madison Square Garden in New York City. Bayley submitted to Valkyria.

During a promo on this past Monday’s Raw, Valkyria interrupted an interview Byron Saxton was doing with Sol Ruca, saying she (Valkyria) ran Bayley out of WWE and was coming for the Women’s Intercontinental Championship.

Bayley was recently featured in advertising for WWE’s upcoming SummerSlam tryout in Minneapolis alongside Shawn Michaels, Nattie, Je’Von Evans, and Robert Stone. However, the OnLocation website has since updated the graphics, replacing Bayley with Ivy Nile.

Unless Bayley has not signed a new deal with WWE, her contract is believed to expire this October.
