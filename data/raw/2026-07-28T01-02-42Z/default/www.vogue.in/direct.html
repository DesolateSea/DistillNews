    Cancer Horoscope Today: July 27, 2026

    See what the stars have in store for your sign
    By Snehaa Khanna Sahgal
    27 July 2026
    Illustration by: Saahir Shetty (@saahir_shetty)

    Face fear and rise, Cancer. This is your theme this week. The past couple of years may have felt as though they were tearing you down, especially the past couple of months, and you chose to slow down and try a new approach—which may or may not have worked. But it gave you time to breathe, offered you a different perspective and helped you realise the things you need to learn, change, evolve through and grow from. There are several paths laid out in front of you and you must choose which way you wish to go. This is not a time to remain passive. This is the time to choose growth.

    Cosmic tip: Nurture yourself but also make bold decisions that move you in your desired direction.
