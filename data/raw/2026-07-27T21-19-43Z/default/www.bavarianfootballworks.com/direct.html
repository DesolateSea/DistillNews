Bayern could be in mix for Barcola, but is it realistic? (L’Equipe via @iMiaSanMia)

Bayern Munich has long been an admirer of Paris Saint-Germain attacker Bradley Barcola. Now, the France international could actually be available, but the Bavarians might not be inclined to outduel Liverpool (the frontrunner), Arsenal, and Chelsea to swing a deal:

Bradley Barcola has decided not to extend his contract at PSG. The clubs that have made their interest known are: Liverpool, Arsenal, Bayern and Chelsea. The latter are no longer in the running. Barcola’s contract runs until 2028. A move this summer depends on the European transfer market for wingers

Liverpool are currently leading the race for Barcola. Bayern admire the player and have followed him for some time. But their ability to make a move for him depends on developments elsewhere in the market

PSG would only consider offers from €120m. @lequipe, @Tanziloic

However, Fabrizio Romano also checked in to confirm the information:

🚨🔴 Liverpool have been leading the race for Bradley Barcola since April — and they are working on the deal.

Understand Barcola’s open to the move and gives priority to #LFC project if they can get a deal done with PSG.

Liverpool, waiting for PSG indications on price/details.

Romano also reported (via @iMiaSanMia) that everything has gone silent with Bayern Munich after an initial buzz:

Bayern made some calls in June to understand Bradley Barcola’s situation in case there were developments around their superstars. Things have gone quiet ever since, and Bayern have not advanced regarding Barcola because they now expect all their superstars to stay at the club.
