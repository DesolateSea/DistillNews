Priyanka Chopra makes Hyderabad airport her latest runway in a monochrome co-ord set, with Nick Jonas and Malti Marie as the perfect style sidekicks

1/4

Priyanka Chopra doesn’t do casual

Long before the cameras start rolling for her upcoming release, 'Varanasi', Priyanka Chopra Jonas has already been making news with her iconic fashion choices. The global celebrity landed in Hyderabad with husband Nick Jonas and daughter Malti Marie, reinforcing once again that she can make a fashion statement at the airport just as easily as she can on the red carpet. Wearing an elegant monochrome ensemble that offered the perfect combination of elegance, simplicity, and comfort, Priyanka used a simple airport arrival to showcase her special take on travel fashion.

2/4

A first-class upgrade to minimal fashion

At Hyderabad airport, Priyanka chose a relaxed and casual outfit set in dark brown hues. She wore an off-shoulder top with matching joggers, forming a comforting co-ord set, and completed the look with off-white statement T-style innerwear. For accessories, she added a beige cap with a pair of black shades and chic brown sandals. With a no-makeup clean look, she was simply seen tying up her hair in a messy low bun and aced the minimal-styling route.


(Image Credits: Instagram)

3/4

Hand-in-hand with Nick Jonas

During the same time, Nick Jonas adhered to his distinct minimalist styling by wearing a black shirt with matching pants and broke the monotone pattern with a pair of black-and-white checkered shoes and tinted sunglasses. Nonetheless, the most adorable one was Malti Marie, who appeared satisfied and at ease in her father's arms, wearing an adorable airport-chic pink ensemble.


(Image Credits: Instagram)

4/4

This moment serves as yet another lesson in luxury dressing.

Priyanka’s recent arrival at the airport showcases how true fashion lies in the perfect finishing of a tailored and timeless colour scheme instead of lavish ornamentation. As she prepares for the shooting of 'Varanasi', her monochromatic airport look confirms that minimal dressing is as powerful as haute couture. Priyanka was spotted at the airport with her husband and daughter but ended up shining the most.
