Headline Roundup

McConnell's Office Shares Photo Update, Gov. Beshear Gives Him an Ultimatum

Various reports on the health status of absent Sen. Mitch McConnell (R-KY) have emerged this week as Kentucky's Democratic Governor Andy Beshear has called for transparency or his resignation.

For Context: McConnell has been hospitalized since June 14, with scarce updates on his health since then. He was rumored to have possibly suffered cardiac arrest and has missed 38 consecutive House votes since his hospitalization.

Photo Evidence: McConnell's office shared a photo of him with his wife on July 26, where he is dressed normally and sitting up in a bed, smiling. Congress's Office of the Attending Physician said he is "not yet medically cleared to leave the rehab facility." McConnell spokesman David Popp said McConnell "continues to meet with staff regarding Senate and Kentucky business, including discussions on defense appropriations, foreign policy, the farm bill, constituent services and constituent federal grant requests." The photo was highlighted by more culture-themed outlets like Forbes (Center), USA Today (Lean Left), The Independent (Lean Left), and People (Lean Left).

Anecdotal Evidence: Independent journalist Nicholas Ballasy, who has contributed to many major mainstream news outlets across the spectrum, reported speaking to Sen. Shelley Moore Capito (R-WV) on July 26, who said McConnell "is recovering and is healing and is in a rehabilitation hospital." She added, "I talked to somebody yesterday who visited him and said that he's clear-minded, and he just needs to get stronger." This report was most notably highlighted by The Hill (Center) and a few other more culture-themed major outlets like Newsweek (Center), The Daily Beast (Left), and People.

Beshear's Ultimatum: Beshear wrote on Monday, "I am calling on you to directly and verbally address the people of Kentucky and provide proof of your capacity to serve, or resign. I am further sending this letter to Senate Majority Leader John Thune. Per our research, a vacancy in the Senate occurs when a senator dies, resigns, or is expelled by a vote of the Senate itself. If you are unwilling to voluntarily show that you still have the capacity to serve, I will insist Leader Thune fully investigate your condition, report it to the American people, and begin the process if warranted." This was covered widely across the spectrum by major outlets.

Media Perspective: An opinion published in MS NOW (Left) played down "conspiracy theories" that the Republican Party may be trying to use McConnell's absence to its political advantage. Lindsay Beyerstein wrote, "The idea that the Republican establishment at large is hiding McConnell's condition makes no sense. Why would the Republicans be scheming to deprive themselves of a Senate seat? And if they were, what Democrat in their right mind would interfere?"
