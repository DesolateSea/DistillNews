How much did Vikrant Massey charge for 'Musafir Cafe'? Salaries of entire cast including Vedika Pinto and Mahima Makwana revealed
Netflix's latest romantic drama 'Musafir Cafe' has been winning praise for its heartfelt storytelling and understated treatment of love. Helmed by Ruchir Arun, the series is adapted from Divya Prakash Dubey's bestselling Hindi novel of the same name and premiered on the streaming platform on Friday.
Featuring Vikrant Massey, Vedika Pinto and Mahima Makwana in pivotal roles, the series was reportedly made on a controlled budget.
As stated by various media reports, Vikrant has emerged as the highest-paid member of the cast, although he also serves as the co-producer of the project under his banner, Homemade Stories.

Reported salaries of the lead cast of 'Musafir Cafe'

As per multiple reports, Vikrant Massey was paid approximately Rs 10 to 15 crore for 'Musafir Cafe'.
Besides portraying Chander Mohan Sharma, the actor has also made his debut as a co-producer with the series.
Vedika Pinto, who essays the role of Sudha, reportedly earned between Rs 3 crore and Rs 5 crore. Mahima Makwana, seen as Preeti, is said to have received around Rs 2 crore to Rs 3 crore for her performance.
Actor Adil Hussain, who plays Mark, reportedly took home between Rs 3 crore and Rs 4 crore. Meanwhile, supporting actors Rajeev Siddhartha, Anubha Fatehpuria, Loveleen Mishra and Sadia Siddiqui were reportedly paid anywhere between Rs 80 lakh and Rs 2 crore, depending on their roles.

'Musafir Cafe': A love story shaped by fate

Produced by Terribly Tiny Tales (TTT) and Homemade Stories, 'Musafir Cafe' has been created and written by Sharanya Rajgopal. The story follows Chander Mohan Sharma, an engineer who leaves behind a successful career in the United States to return to Bhopal. Under pressure from his family, he meets Sudha, an independent divorce lawyer who has little interest in marriage and is focused on achieving her personal ambitions.
Although the two develop a connection, life takes an unexpected turn. Chander eventually moves to Mussoorie, where he opens a cafÃ© and begins a new chapter with Preeti, whose calm and reserved nature stands in sharp contrast to Sudha's personality. The narrative explores how Chander navigates love, loss and second chances when his past unexpectedly resurfaces.
