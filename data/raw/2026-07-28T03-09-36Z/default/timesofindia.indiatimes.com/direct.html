Apoorva Mukhija leaves her Mumbai home after 'India's Got Latent' controversy: 'End of an era'

TOI Entertainment Desk / etimes.in / Apr 17, 2025, 07:57 IST
Share
AA
Text Size
  • Small
  • Medium
  • Large
YouTuber Apoorva Mukhija, known as The Rebel Kid, faced backlash after appearing on Samay Raina's show with Ranveer Allahbadia, leading to online threats and harassment. Following the controversy and an apology, she deleted her content and hinted at moving out of her Mumbai apartment.
Youtuber Apoorva Mukhija was surrounded amidst controversies after her appearance on 'India's Got Latent'. She made an appearance on Samay Raina's show with Ranveer Allahbadia whose comment did not go down well. Ranveer asked a contestant on the show, "Would you rather see your parents have sex for the rest of your life or jump in once and stop it forever?" Meanwhile, some of Apoorva aka The Rebel Kid's comments also were not appreciated.
After this whole controversy, Apoorva had apologised and she finally made her comeback on the gram after deleting all her previous content. And now she dropped a picture which hinted that she's moving out of her Mumbai apartment. She shared a picture of a house with dim light, cardboard boxes and cleaning material. She captioned this picture as, "End Of An Era."
Meanwhile, Apoorva also revealed through her social media that she has been getting a lot of death threats, rape threats on the internet after this whole incident. She said, "I started reading my DMs, and they were all graphic of how people want to rape me, throw acid and stuff like that." She also added that she cried so much because of how the paparazzi behaved with her when she went to record her statement with the police and she called it a 'dehumanising' experience. She added, "I deserve this because I did this. My parents did not deserve this."
Meanwhile, while giving her apology, Apoorva had said, "I have hurt a lot of people and that is honestly the last thing I want to do. I genuinely create content to entertain people and to make them laugh. I never wanted to hurt anybody and I am very very sorry. I should have been more mindful of my words but I have learnt my lesson and I promise I will do better going forward. I just hope you guys can forgive me and I am very sorry if I hurt your sentiments
