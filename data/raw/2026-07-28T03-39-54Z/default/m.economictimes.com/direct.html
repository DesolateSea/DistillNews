    Synopsis

    Easter 2025 will be observed on Sunday, April 20. Here's a guide to what services and businesses in the U.S. will be open or closed in observance of this major Christian festival.

    Easter 2025, commemorating the resurrection of Jesus Christ, will be observed across the United States on Sunday, April 20.

    The date shifts each year due to its connection with the ecclesiastical approximation of the spring equinox and the Paschal full moon.

    As one of Christianity’s most sacred festivals, it follows the 40-day Lenten period and concludes Holy Week, beginning with Palm Sunday and ending on Easter.


    Though Easter Sunday holds profound religious significance, it is not classified as a federal holiday in the U.S. Nevertheless, many businesses and public institutions modify operations in deference to the day’s cultural and spiritual weight.

    Good Friday 2025: Religious Significance, Not a Federal Holiday

    Live Events


        This year, Good Friday—marking the crucifixion of Jesus—is observed on April 18, 2025.

        While deeply meaningful within Christian traditions, Good Friday is not a federal holiday in the United States. Accordingly, government offices, postal services, and most financial institutions remain open.

        However, some private employers and state governments, particularly in areas with large Christian populations, may provide a day off or reduced hours.

        Are Banks Open on Easter Sunday?

        Banks nationwide will generally remain closed on Easter Sunday, consistent with their usual Sunday schedules.

        Major institutions such as Bank of America, Wells Fargo, JPMorgan Chase, Citibank, and PNC will not operate on April 20. Customers are advised to check with their local branches for any exceptions.

        Capital One has indicated that its branches and cafés will observe regular business hours on Good Friday, though most will not open on Easter Sunday itself.

        USPS, FedEx, and UPS: Easter Sunday Operations

        The U.S. Postal Service (USPS) will not deliver regular mail on Easter Sunday, April 20, in line with its standard Sunday protocol. Retail counters at USPS locations will also be closed.

        However, Priority Mail Express services will continue to operate on Easter, as they do throughout the year.

        FedEx and UPS will likewise suspend standard pickup and delivery services on Easter.

        According to official updates, most FedEx Office locations will modify hours, while UPS Store branches may be closed entirely.

        Customers requiring urgent shipments may turn to FedEx Custom Critical or UPS Express Critical, both of which run year-round, including holidays.

        What’s Open, What’s Closed: Grocery Stores and Restaurants

        Many grocery stores and restaurants will either reduce hours or close entirely on Easter.

        Chains often make decisions on a location-by-location basis, and closures are more common in regions with large Christian communities.

        Customers should contact local stores in advance to confirm availability.

        National retailers and pharmacies with historically limited Easter operations may adjust hours for 2025.

        In previous years, some outlets such as Costco and Trader Joe’s remained closed on Easter, while others like Walmart and CVS operated on shortened schedules.

        Easter Sunday: A Quiet Yet Reverent Day Across the U.S.

        Although not a federal holiday, Easter 2025 is widely acknowledged with closures, reduced services, and moments of solemn observance across the country.

        With most government services closed and limited retail activity, the day serves as a reflective occasion for many Americans, combining faith, family gatherings, and springtime traditions.

        FAQs

        When is Easter Sunday 2025?
        Easter is celebrated on the first Sunday following the full moon that occurs on or after the spring equinox. In 2025, the spring equinox took place on Thursday, March 20, placing Easter Sunday on April 20.

        Why is Easter 2025 so late?
        Because the full moon is considered to occur on Sunday, April 13, Easter cannot be celebrated on that day. Therefore, the observance is postponed to the next Sunday—April 20, 2025.
