Arshdeep Singh introduces Samreen Kaur as 'my person', here's everything to know about the actor-model and former beauty pageant finalist

TOI Entertainment Desk / etimes.in / Jul 27, 2026, 13:37 IST
Arshdeep Singh introduces Samreen Kaur as 'my person', here's everything to know about the actor-model and former beauty pageant finalist
Indian pacer Arshdeep Singh has finally put an end to months of speculation surrounding his personal life by making his relationship with actor-model Samreen Kaur official on Instagram. Sharing a pair of affectionate pictures, the cricketer introduced Samreen as "my person," delighting fans and fellow sportspersons alike.
The post quickly gained massive traction, collecting millions of likes and congratulatory messages.
According to SCREEN, born on September 7, 1999, Samreen Kaur is originally from Jammu and Kashmir. She first grabbed attention after representing her state as a finalist at the Femina Miss India 2018 pageant. Following the competition, she pursued a career in modelling while completing her B.Com (Honours) from Symbiosis College of Arts and Commerce in Pune.
She later transitioned into acting, making her screen debut with Kabir Khan's sports drama '83'. Over the years, she has expanded her portfolio with appearances in the ZEE5 series 'Nail Polish' and the Punjabi film 'Sardaar Ji 2'.

Samreen Kaur a popular face in music videos and digital content

Apart from films and web projects, Samreen has featured in several popular music videos alongside artists such as Badshah, Guru Randhawa, Jubin Nautiyal, Jass Manak and Maninder Buttar. These collaborations helped her build a strong fan following among younger audiences.

She is also active on social media, where she regularly posts fashion, travel and lifestyle content, steadily establishing herself as a rising name in the entertainment industry.

Earlier linked to Ashish Chanchlani

Before her relationship with Arshdeep Singh became public, Samreen was frequently linked to YouTuber Ashish Chanchlani. However, both had denied dating rumours and maintained that they were close friends.
In a later interaction, Samreen acknowledged that their friendship had evolved over time due to personal circumstances, while stressing that they continued to respect one another.

Arshdeep Singh makes relationship official

Speculation about Samreen and Arshdeep's romance first gained momentum during the IPL 2026 season when she was seen cheering for the Punjab Kings fast bowler from the stands. The pair was also spotted together on multiple occasions, further fuelling rumours.
Although neither addressed the reports at the time, Arshdeep's latest Instagram post has now officially confirmed that the two are in a relationship, ending weeks of speculation among fans.
