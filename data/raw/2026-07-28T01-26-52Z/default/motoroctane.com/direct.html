How To Get A BMW For Just Rs 3 Lakh

By Prathamesh Shanbhag
July 27, 2026
Share

    It is a dream of many enthusiasts to have a performance luxury car parked in their garage. But the luxury badge comes with a hefty price tag. However, here’s a chance for such enthusiasts to fulfil this dream, as we have come across a BMW that costs even less than a Maruti Suzuki Alto. Here are all the details.
    Attention Readers! Join us on the WhatsApp Community for daily auto news updates.

    - Advertisement -

    Cheapest BMW Car

    Her Garage, a YouTuber, posted a video showcasing a youth modifying his Honda Amaze into a BMW M4 to satisfy his dream of owning a luxury car. The key highlight of the video is the modification cost – which is three lakh, while the actual M4 is priced in crores.

    Speaking about the modification, the modifier explains how he actually shaped the Amaze to look like an M4. The modifier did most of the work on cardboard. He designed the panels, fascia, and the rear exactly how he wanted with cardboard before creating moulds for these body parts.

    - Advertisement -

    One can easily spot the changes, right? The BMW-inspired grille, front bumper, and headlamps. The model retains the bonnet design but gets some fibre design elements to make it look sportier. The car also gets fibre fenders to make it look wider. Other notable changes on the model include a new ORVM design and new side door handles. At the rear, there’s an aggressive-looking roof-mounted spoiler and a new bumper.

    - Advertisement -

    Interior

    While there are a lot of changes on the outside, the modifier has made minimal changes inside. The dashboard has been updated with a new dual-tone black-and-tan colour theme. The seats are kept the same, but the modifier wishes to change them to match the dashboard’s theme. The steering, AC controls, buttons, and other interior items look stock.

    NOTE: While the car modification might seem like an attractive idea, it can penalise you. If you plan to modify your car, consult your local RTO before making such customisations, as most car modifications are illegal in India.
