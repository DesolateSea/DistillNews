Sony sets February 2027 release for God of War: Laufey, confirms Kratos sequel is in development

The PlayStation maker unveiled the launch date for God of War: Laufey at San Diego Comic-Con 2026 and confirmed that the next chapter in Kratos' story will continue directly from the spin-off.

Sony sets February 2027 release for God of War: Laufey, confirms Kratos sequel is in development

ADVERTISEMENT

Sony has announced that God of War: Laufey will launch exclusively on PlayStation 5 on February 16, 2027, while also confirming that a new mainline God of War game starring Kratos is currently in development.

The announcements were made during San Diego Comic-Con 2026, where Santa Monica Studio shared fresh details about the expanding God of War universe. The upcoming Kratos title will serve as a direct continuation of the events depicted in God of War: Laufey, according to studio creative lead Cory Barlog.

First revealed in June, God of War: Laufey shifts the spotlight away from Kratos and Atreus to follow Faye, also known as Laufey, whose story unfolds alongside the events of the 2018 God of War reboot.

The game takes players into the afterlife of the Norse gods, where Faye discovers that the plans she had set in motion to protect Kratos and Atreus are under threat. Determined to safeguard her family, she embarks on a journey through the realm of the dead while confronting powerful enemies.

Faye is voiced by Deborah Ann Woll, best known for her performances in True Blood and Daredevil. Although the character played a pivotal role throughout the Norse saga, her story largely remained in the background because the series begins after her death. The new title places her at the centre of the narrative for the first time.

Santa Monica Studio said the gameplay has been built to distinguish Faye from previous protagonists while preserving the combat identity of the franchise.

Grace Orlady, Communications Director at Santa Monica Studio, said the development team designed Faye's combat style to reflect her strength while introducing a fresh gameplay experience. According to the studio, the game combines the fast-paced movement associated with the original Greek-era God of War titles with the deeper storytelling and world-building introduced during the Norse saga.

Sony also confirmed that God of War: Laufey will receive a physical PlayStation 5 release. However, the company did not provide a launch window for the newly announced Kratos sequel, indicating only that it will continue the story established in Laufey.

While few details about the next mainline entry were revealed, the announcement signals that Santa Monica Studio intends to expand the franchise beyond Kratos' original Norse storyline by linking future games to Faye's previously untold journey.
