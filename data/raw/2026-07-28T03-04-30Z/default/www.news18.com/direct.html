Hardik Pandya’s ex’wife Natasa Stankovic is being trolled online for allowing her son, Agastya, to sit in the front row at a fashion show in Mumbai, with many claiming it’s not age-appropriate. The actress walked the ramp at the event on Saturday, while her son was seated in the audience alongside Aleksandar Alex. Aleksandar, who is both a model and fitness coach, was seen affectionately caring for Agastya—at one point even kissing him on the head—sparking a wave of reactions online. A video of the moment has since gone viral. While there has been speculation that Natasa and Aleksandar are dating, several Instagram users have claimed that he is actually her cousin brother.

A netizen took to the comments section to write, “Why to bring kids to such events ? Just because her mother is walking the ramp? The kid has no idea ki ho kya raha hai ?" Another wrote, “As much as I support Natasa, the fashion show is not age-appropriate for her son." Many also rallied behind her and said that she’s taking good care of the son.

Natasa Stankovic and Hardik Pandya relationship

Natasa Stankovic and cricketer Hardik Pandya got married in May 2020 during the COVID-19 lockdown. They later had a grand wedding celebration in February 2023 in Udaipur, where they renewed their vows. The couple has a son named Agastya, who was born in July 2020. Over the years, they have shared many happy moments together on social media. However, there were rumours about problems in their marriage, especially since Natasa has been seen without Hardik at several events.

In July 2024, Natasa and Hardik announced separation. The couple issued an official statement and confirmed that they are no longer together. They mentioned that it was a “tough decision" for both of them and shared that they will continue to co-parent their 3-year-old son, Agastya.

“After 4 years of being together, Hardik and I have decided to mutually part ways. We tried our best together and gave it our all, and we believe this is in the best interest for the both of us. This was a tough decision for us to make, given the joy, mutual respect and companionship we enjoyed together and as we grew a family," the statement read.
