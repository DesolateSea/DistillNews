iQOO Z11 Lite Launched in India with High Capacity Battery
The iQOO Z11 Lite is now available in India, featuring a 6500mAh battery and advanced AI capabilities at an accessible price.

iQOO has officially introduced its latest budget smartphone, the iQOO Z11 Lite, targeting power users in India. The device is designed for those prioritising exceptional battery life, featuring a substantial 6500mAh power cell that aims to deliver prolonged usage.

Equipped with the MediaTek Dimensity 6300 processor, which is manufactured on a 6nm process, the iQOO Z11 Lite promises reliable performance. Users can choose up to 6GB of LPDDR4x RAM and also benefit from an additional 6GB of virtual RAM, offering ample multitasking capabilities. Storage options allow expansion of up to 1TB, providing sufficient space for apps, media, and more.

Running on OriginOS 6 based on Android 16, iQOO has pledged to deliver two major operating system upgrades alongside four years of security updates for the 6GB RAM variant. This commitment aims to ensure that users enjoy ongoing improvements and enhancements over time.

One of the key selling points of the iQOO Z11 Lite is its advanced artificial intelligence features, which iQOO refers to as the âNon-Stop Intelligenceâ software suite. This suite incorporates capabilities typically found in higher-end devices. For photography, the smartphone houses a dual rear camera setup, featuring a 50MP primary sensor, which utilizes either the Sony IMX852 or LYT505C sensor, depending on the production batch. This sensor is paired with a 0.08MP depth sensor for enhanced image quality. Additionally, there is a 5MP front camera for selfies and video calls, conveniently located in a waterdrop notch.

The iQOO Z11 Lite comes in two appealing hues: Midnight Blue and Solar Flame. It is set to hit retail markets on July 30th, available through Amazon India, the iQOO India online store, and authorised physical retailers. To promote sales, iQOO is offering a discount of â¹1,500 for early purchasers. Buyers using ICICI and HDFC Bank cards can avail this discount through the iQOO store, while those shopping on Amazon can benefit from similar discounts using Axis and SBI cards. Furthermore, a three-month no-cost EMI option is available for buyers.

With a luminous display offering 1200 nits of brightness and IP65 water and dust resistance, the iQOO Z11 Lite stands out as a highly functional option for gig workers and users who often find themselves away from chargers. Although the display has a resolution of 720p, the combination of its extensive battery life, AI-driven features, and a solid 50MP camera makes it a compelling choice for budget-conscious consumers looking for dependable performance.
