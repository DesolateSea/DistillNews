Recent fact checks

Restore Britain leader Rupert Lowe MP and others have claimed it is established that 250,000 children have been victims of so–called “rape gangs”.

This estimate is not calculated reliably. It is derived from unevidenced assumptions, and partly based on figures for child sexual exploitation in general, not by rape gangs specifically.

Unreliable estimate

Earlier this year Conservative MPs started saying that welfare payments had just overtaken income tax receipts “for the first time ever”.

It’s not true. The party has said it would correct this error, but almost a month later it still hasn’t done so.

Correction watch

An image apparently shows damage to a Ryanair plane after a passenger was almost sucked out of a window mid-flight.

The image is not genuine. It is an AI-generated fake, and doesn’t match genuine photos and videos of the incident.
