    Sensex, Nifty stock market prediction next week: Bulls to lead share bazaar? Support, resistance, trading view

    Sensex, Nifty stock market prediction next week: Despite a sharp volatility in the global equity markets, the NSE Nifty 50 and Sensex each rallied around 6 per cent in the past week as markets resumed the risk-off sentiment.     Updated Apr 17, 2025 21:40 IST

    Photo : ET Now Digital

    Sensex, Nifty stock market prediction next week: Bulls to lead share bazaar? Support, resistance, trading view

    Sensex, Nifty stock market prediction next week: While the trading was open for just three days this week (April 15-17), domestic benchmark indices recovered majority of the losses made after US President Donald Trump’s April 2 reciprocal tariff announcement. This week especially was marked by a big bull run.
    India’s stock markets have shown a remarkable resilience despite recent global uncertainties, Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman said on Thursday. With this, she even noted the “increasingly central role” domestic investors have played in stabilising the markets.
    Despite a sharp volatility in the global equity markets, the NSE Nifty 50 and Sensex each rallied around 6 per cent in the past week as markets resumed the risk-off sentiment. The mid-cap index also gained around 6 per cent and small-cap index climbed around 8 per cent.
    This week also brought good news for all the indices which ended with massive gains. For example, the realty index was up 8.9 per cent, telecom zoomed 8 per cent, capital goods rose by 6.8 per cent, to name a few. Will the bulls continue their run in the coming week too? Here’s what market experts suggest.

    Sensex, Nifty Stock Market Prediction Next Week

    The Q4 quarterly results announcement season has started and in the coming week too, investors' attention will be on the Q4FY25 earnings season. The volatility index, India VIX, cooled off sharply by 2.50 per cent to 15.47, indicating reduced market volatility.
    Kotak Securities’ Amol Athawale explained that during the week, the market successfully surpassed the 20-day and 50-day SMA (Simple Moving Average) zones, which is largely positive. “On the weekly charts, a bullish candle was formed, and the market is holding an uptrend continuation formation on both daily and intraday charts.”
    This supports further upward movement from current levels. The market expert went on to add that the short-term market texture appears bullish. “However, due to temporary overbought conditions, we may see range-bound activity in the near future.”
    From a technical perspective, the Nifty has formed a strong bullish candle on both the daily and weekly charts, indicating underlying strength, said Hrishikesh Yedve of Asit C. Mehta Investment Interrmediates. Sustaining above the 23,900 level could pave the way for an extended rally towards 24,050, he added

    Sensex, Nifty Support and Resistance Levels

    For traders, the levels of 23,500/77400 and 23,350/76900 would act as key support zones, while resistance areas for the bulls could be found between 24,000/79000 and 24,200/79600. However, if the market dips below 23,350/76900, sentiment could change, prompting traders to consider exiting their long positions, shared Athawale.
    Yedve added that a decisive move above 23,900 could open the door for a further upmove towards 24,200 for Nifty 50. On the downside, immediate support lies at 23,800, followed by 23,500. As long as these levels hold, a 'buy on dips' approach remains advisable.
