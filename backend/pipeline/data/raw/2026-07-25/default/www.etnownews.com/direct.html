Indian Oil dividend announcement 2025 in Q4 results; date, schedule fixed | Quarterly earnings

Written by: ET Now Digital

Updated Apr 19, 2025 10:34 IST

Indian Oil Dividend Announcement 2025: Indian Oil dividend announcement is set to take place in Q4 results FY2025. Indian Oil dividend record date may also be announced along with amount. Indian Oil dividend yield is nearly 9 per cent on each stock.

3 Mins Read
Follow
Indian Oil Dividend 2025 Announcement: Indian Oil shareholders could soon a good news from the company's board. The state-run company has announced that dividend announcement is scheduled to take place in the quarterly results (Q4 results FY2025). Indian Oil dividend record date announcement could also take place along with amount in the Q4 results. Indian Oil dividend yield nearly 9 per cent.

Indian Oil dividend announcement 2025 in Q4 results; date, schedule fixed (Pic: ET NOW)

According to an exchange filing by Indian Oil on Thursday (April 17), a board meeting is schedule next week to announce quarterly results (Q4 FY2025). Indian Oil said that its board could declare a dividend in the Q4 results.

Indian Oil Quarterly Results

Indian Oil board meting is scheduled to take place on April 30 to consider and approve the financial statement for the fourth quarter and also FY 2025, it said in a filing. Indian Oil board will also take up a proposal for recommending dividend for shareholders. If announced, then this could be a final dividend by Indian Oil for FY 2025.
"It is hereby informed that a Board Meeting of the Company is scheduled on Wednesday, 30" April 2025, inter alia, to consider and approve Standalone as well as Consolidated Audited Financial Results of the Company for the quarter and year ended 31 March 2025," Indian Oil said in filing.

Indian Oil Dividend Announcement 2025

"It is also hereby informed that Board may consider declaration of dividend for the financial year 2024-25, if any, at the aforesaid Board Meeting," Indian Oil added in the same filing.

Indian Oil Dividend 2025 Record Date

Indian Oil didn't announce dividend record date in the latest exchange filing. It likely that Indian Oil board could declare the dividend record date along with among in the Q4 results on April 30.

Indian Oil Dividednd Yield

Indian Oil is a top dividend paying stock. Indian Oil dividend yield is 8.95 per cent on each stock.

Indian Oil Dividend History

Indian Oil has a good record of paying dividend to its shareholders. The final dividend announcement by Indian Oil is scheduled to take place on April 30. In 2024, Indian Oil paid a whopping dividend of Rs 7 on each stock to its investors. In 2023, Indian Oil distributed a dividend of Rs 5 in November and Rs 3 in July. Back in 2022, Indian Oil had paid dividend on two occasions, aggregating to Rs 6.40 on each stock and issued bonus shares in the ratio of 1:2.
